# Project Ops Research

A Codex skill for project operations research, competitor discovery, SEO analysis, paid ads analysis, and go-to-market strategy.

## What It Does

Give this skill an industry, niche, product, website category, or business idea. It will research the market, identify strong competitors, analyze their websites and acquisition channels, and produce an actionable operations report.

The skill is designed for:

- Industry and product research
- Competitor discovery
- SEO structure and keyword analysis
- Google Ads and paid acquisition analysis
- Website technology stack checks
- Promotion and operations model analysis
- 30/60/90-day go-to-market planning

## Example Prompts

```text
Use project-ops-research to research the DIY natural incense cone kit market.
```

```text
帮我做一个关于XXXX的项目运营调研，找竞对并分析 SEO 和投放模式。
```

```text
Analyze competitors for a Shopify product idea and recommend a 30/60/90-day go-to-market plan.
```

## Output

The skill produces a structured report covering:

- Market overview
- 3-5 competitor websites
- Company, domain, and business model analysis
- Traffic channel diagnosis
- SEO or paid ads breakdown
- Cross-competitor patterns
- Actionable market entry recommendations

## Installation

Copy this folder into your Codex skills directory:

```text
~/.codex/skills/project-ops-research
```

On Windows:

```text
C:\Users\<YourName>\.codex\skills\project-ops-research
```

Then start a new Codex conversation and ask for a project operations, industry, or competitor research task.
