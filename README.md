# Project Ops Research

A Codex skill for overseas project operations research, competitor discovery, SEO analysis, paid ads analysis, promotion SOPs, and PPTX go-to-market strategy decks.

## What It Does

Give this skill an industry, niche, product, website category, competitor company, or competitor URL. It researches the overseas market, identifies or deep-dives competitors, analyzes their websites and acquisition channels, and produces a decision-ready PPTX presentation.

The skill is designed for:

- Overseas industry and product research
- Competitor discovery from Google search results
- Provided competitor URL/company deep dives
- Company, domain age, business model, and website stack analysis
- SEO structure, keyword, and traffic analysis
- Google Ads and paid acquisition analysis
- Marketplace, social, influencer, affiliate, and distributor channel analysis
- Promotion SOPs with expected outcomes
- 30/60/90-day go-to-market planning

## Example Prompts

```text
Use project-ops-research to research the DIY natural incense cone kit market and output a PPT.
```

```text
帮我做一个关于天然塔香的出海项目运营调研，输出 PPT，分析海外市场、竞对、SEO、投流和起步推广 SOP。
```

```text
Analyze veilincense.com as a competitor and create a PPT with company, domain, traffic, SEO, paid ads, operating model, and entry recommendations.
```

## Output

The skill produces a `.pptx` deck covering:

- Overseas market overview
- Demand and trend evidence
- Google/SERP competitor discovery or provided competitor deep dive
- Competitor company, domain, offer, traffic, and channel comparison
- SEO structure and keyword analysis
- Paid traffic and ROI hypothesis
- Marketplace, social, influencer, affiliate, or distributor analysis
- Entry requirements
- Promotion SOPs by channel
- 30/60/90-day action plan
- Budget, expected outcomes, risks, and source appendix

## Installation

Copy this folder into your Codex skills directory:

```text
~/.codex/skills/project-ops-research
```

On Windows:

```text
C:\Users\<YourName>\.codex\skills\project-ops-research
```

Then start a new Codex conversation and ask for an industry, product, or competitor research task.
