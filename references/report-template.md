# PPT Deck Template: Project Operations Research

Use this template when producing the required PPTX deck. Adapt slide count to the complexity of the industry, but keep the order.

## Slide 1: Cover

- Title: `[Industry/Product] Overseas Operations Research`
- Subtitle: market overview, competitor analysis, promotion strategy, and entry recommendation
- Date
- Research scope and target market

## Slide 2: Executive conclusion

- One-sentence market judgment
- Whether the opportunity is worth entering
- Best entry angle
- Recommended primary channel
- Required capabilities
- Biggest risk

## Slide 3: Research scope and assumptions

- Product/category definition
- Target country/language
- Customer segment
- Included channels
- Data sources used
- Confidence level and limitations

## Slide 4: Overseas macro environment

- Current market status
- Demand drivers
- Buyer use cases
- Price bands
- Main purchase channels
- Seasonality, compliance, logistics, cultural, or trust barriers

## Slide 5: Market trend and demand evidence

- Search trend or keyword trend signals
- Marketplace demand signals
- Social/content trend signals
- Industry report or authoritative source signals
- Summary of what the evidence means

## Slide 6: SERP and competitor discovery

Use this slide when the user did not provide competitors.

- Search terms used
- First 8 Google result pages or equivalent search scope
- Count of real competitors found
- Result type mix: brands, marketplaces, directories, blogs, review sites, ads
- Competitors selected for deep dive and why

## Slide 7: Provided competitor scope

Use this slide when the user provided competitor companies or URLs.

- Provided targets
- Added benchmark competitors if any
- Why each target matters
- Research limitations for each target

Create one slide per important competitor.

- Evidence links
- What is worth learning
- Weaknesses or gaps
